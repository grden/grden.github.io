Leading a five-person team as PM, I redesigned the user flow of <ArrowLink href="https://www.riido.io/" className="font-normal">Riido</ArrowLink>, SWYG's AI project management tool, to improve its usability.

<br/>

## Problem

While Riido started as a tool to alleviate project management burdens, current feedback indicates that the platform suffers from complicated user flows and low UX consistency.
  
Our initial analysis identified the AI project schedule creation process as the primary bottleneck, where excessive complication prevents users from intuitively achieving their goals.

<br/>

## User Research

To further investigate and confirm the problem, we conducted **Usability Tests with 10 different users**.

Participants were assigned role-based scenarios and asked to complete four tasks.

**Key Takeaways**
<section className="grid grid-cols-2 w-full mx-auto gap-4 md:gap-16">
    <div>
        👩‍💻 Participants familiar with AI-based tools prioritized control.  
        <p className="text-gray-500">They requested structured input flows, the ability to manually adjust results, and transparency regarding the logic behind the AI's output.</p>
    </div>
    <div>
        🧑‍🎓 Participants less familiar with AI tools emphasized clarity.  
        <p className="text-gray-500">They struggled with low UI visibility and a lack of immediate feedback regarding system status or errors.</p>
    </div>
</section>
<p className="text-gray-500">All participants struggled to intuitively identify the platform's primary purpose and role upon first use.</p>

## A/B Test
To validate our solution, we conducted A/B Tests with 10 participants.
